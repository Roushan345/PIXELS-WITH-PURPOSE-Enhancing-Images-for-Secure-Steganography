📐 Tier 2 Architecture & Security Pipeline

01

Payload Preprocessing

Text messages or secret image binary bytes are compressed using zlib (level 9), reducing payload size and removing repetitive entropy patterns.

02

AES-256-CBC Encryption

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 derives a 256-bit encryption key with 100,000 iterations and a cryptographically secure 16-byte random salt and IV.

03

HMAC-SHA256 Integrity

Encrypt-then-MAC authentication tag verifies zero tampering or bit alteration. If even 1 pixel is corrupted, the tamper alarm triggers.

04

PRNG Bit Dispersion

Bits are scattered non-sequentially across selected channels (e.g. HVS Blue channel) using a seeded PRNG, bypassing chi-square steganalysis.

👁️ Why Blue Channel & HVS (Human Visual System)?

The human retina contains approximately 64% red cones, 32% green cones, and only 2% to 7% blue cones. As a result, the human visual system is least sensitive to high-frequency spatial luminance variations in the blue spectrum. Embedding payloads specifically into the blue channel achieves maximum stealth and visual imperceptibility.

🔍 Steganalysis Principles (Chi-Square & PoVs)

01

Pairs of Values (PoVs)

In natural images, adjacent even-odd pixel pairs (2k, 2k+1) have varying counts. Sequential LSB embedding tends to equalize these frequencies.

02

Chi-Square Statistic

$\chi^2 = \sum \frac{(Observed - Expected)^2}{Expected}$. Low $\chi^2$ indicates an artificial distribution caused by sequential embedding.

03

p-Value Threshold

A $p$-value < 0.05 indicates statistical confidence that hidden sequential bits exist in the image.

04

Anti-Forensics Defense

By scattering bits non-sequentially with AES pseudo-randomness, the global PoV balance is preserved and detection is evaded.
